2024 – presentMigration · pro bono

Moving an NGO off Azure SQL

Problem
Va Ajutam din Dej, a local NGO, had its members, posts and projects in an Azure SQL database and needed a stack its small volunteer team could run.
What I built
A Node.js import script that moved members, posts and projects into Firestore, a rebuilt Next.js and TypeScript site, Vercel hosting behind Cloudflare DNS, and Google Workspace for Nonprofits for the team's email.
Result
The NGO's data and site now live on Firestore and Vercel, with the team on its own nonprofit Google Workspace.
Stack
Next.js · TypeScript · Node.js · Firestore · Vercel · Cloudflare
